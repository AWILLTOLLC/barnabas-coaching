## The Product

**1" Scotch-Eye Auger Bit**
- Material: High carbon steel (AISI 1075 or equivalent)
- Finish: Black oxide
- Geometry: Helical flute, lead screw tip, welded scotch-eye socket (forged ring eye for wooden handle)
- Target quantity: 1,500–2,000 units
- Budget: $8–15/unit FOB
- Prior sales: ~5,000+ units sold, 30+ countries (used in outreach for credibility)

The scotch-eye socket is the trick part. It's a welded forged ring that accepts a standard wooden handle. Not every tool factory can do this — it's the key filter in outreach.

Previous Chinese factory had consistent communication failures. Manufacturing quality wasn't the core problem — responsiveness and clarity were. We're not blacklisting China, but non-China is preferred: Taiwan, India, Vietnam, Mexico, Eastern Europe, USA.

Pre-shipment QC via QIMA is planned for whatever factory we land on.
