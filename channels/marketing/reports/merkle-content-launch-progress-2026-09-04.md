## Content Highlights

### Healthcare Service Page
**Key insight:** "Traditional IT support sees tickets. Project vendors see scopes. But healthcare labs need someone who understands the difference between CLIA and CAP requirements."

**Use cases:**
- GxP compliance systems (LIMS, electronic lab notebooks, instrument connectivity)
- Patient records integration (EHR interoperability, data integrity)
- Lab instrument networks (mass spectrometers, HPLC, automated sample processors)

**ROI claims:**
- 40% fewer unplanned outages in year one
- Audit prep goes from "where's the documentation?" to "here's what changed last quarter"
- One accountable owner means fewer "that's their problem" moments

### Finance Service Page
**Key insight:** "IT in finance isn't about tickets and tickets closed. It's about understanding how a system change affects your SEC reporting."

**Use cases:**
- SEC compliance systems (FINRA rules, Rule 17a-4, audit trails)
- Trading system infrastructure (uptime during market hours, client reporting)
- Client data management (HIPAA + SEC + confidentiality balance)

**ROI claims:**
- 35% fewer system incidents affecting trading or client reporting
- Audit season goes from panic to "we've got this"
- Faster onboarding: new analysts up in days, not weeks

### Legal Service Page
**Key insight:** "Legal firms run on documents, deadlines, and client confidentiality. Your IT systems need to support all three without getting in the way of the actual work—billable hours."

**Use cases:**
- Discovery systems (document management, annotation tools, production software)
- Client data security (confidentiality as operational reality, not just a requirement)
- Billing system integration (time tracking, matter management, billing flow)

**ROI claims:**
- 45% fewer system incidents during critical filing periods
- Faster onboarding for new associates
- One accountable owner for vendor coordination

### Manufacturing Service Page
**Key insight:** "Modern manufacturing isn't just about machines and materials. It's about the IT systems that connect your production floor to your business operations."

**Use cases:**
- IoT/OT systems integration (sensors, PLCs, SCADA, ERP)
- Supply chain visibility (suppliers, warehouses, distribution centers)
- Production monitoring (real-time visibility, quality metrics, throughput)

**ROI claims:**
- 50% fewer unplanned outages in year one
- Fewer data errors in quality management system = fewer costly recalls
- Faster scaling when adding new production lines or facilities

### Blog Post: Embedded IT vs. Managed Services
**Key insight:** "A vendor sees your IT as a portfolio of systems to keep running. An owner sees it as a business asset that needs to move forward."

**Comparison:**
- Managed services: "Ticket closed" vs. Embedded IT: "Here's what we found"
- Managed services: "That's not in scope" vs. Embedded IT: "Here's what we're doing about it"
- Hidden costs: "not in scope" tax, documentation gap, context problem

### Blog Post: Hidden Costs of Break/Fix IT
**Key insight:** "Your IT is cheap until it's not."

**Cost examples by industry:**
- Healthcare: $10,000–$50,000 per incident (spoiled samples, delayed results, audit findings)
- Finance: $20,000–$100,000+ per incident (missed opportunities, client frustration)
- Legal: $5,000–$25,000 per incident (delayed filings, missed opportunities)
- Manufacturing: $15,000–$75,000 per incident (quality issues, wasted materials)

**Case study:** Lab that lost 3 weeks of data due to break/fix IT. Invoice: $3,500. Real cost: $150,000.

### Blog Post: How Embedded IT Reduces Downtime by 40%
**Key insight:** "40% isn't a magic number. Here's the methodology. Here's what we're measuring. Here's what actually changed."

**Methodology:**
- Baseline: Count outages, duration, systems affected, customer impact
- After embedded IT: Same metrics + problems caught before outages + upgrades during low-impact windows

**Actual numbers from anonymized clients:**
- Biotech lab (60 employees): 79% reduction in downtime hours, 73% reduction in cost
- Financial services (45 employees): 75% reduction in downtime hours, 71% reduction in cost
- Law firm (80 attorneys): 70% reduction in downtime hours, 55% reduction in cost

### Blog Post: Embedded IT for Lab Operations
**Key insight:** "In a lab, IT isn't about helping people check email. It's about keeping research moving."

**Lab-specific challenges:**
- GxP compliance (audit trails, controlled access, version history)
- Instrument connectivity (isolated VLANs, legacy protocols, expiring support contracts)
- Data integrity (corrupted = re-run experiments, lost = months gone)
- 24/7 availability (research doesn't stop at 5pm)

**Specific systems:**
- LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System)
- ELN (Electronic Lab Notebook)
- QMS (Quality Management System)
- Instrument networks
