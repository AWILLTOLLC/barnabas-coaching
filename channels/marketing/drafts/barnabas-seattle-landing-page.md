### AI Consulting for Seattle Small Businesses

You've been hearing about AI for two years. You've probably tried a few things. Maybe some of it stuck, most of it didn't, and you're not sure what you're missing or whether you're missing anything at all.

That's where we come in.

### What we do

Barnabas Coaching helps Seattle-area small businesses work out what AI is actually worth doing for their specific situation.

Not in general. Not based on what worked for someone else's company. For yours.

We start with an AI Audit. We spend time understanding your business, your workflows, and where your people are spending time they shouldn't have to. Then we tell you what AI can help with, what it can't, and what the realistic path forward looks like. No software to sell. No vendor relationships. No agenda beyond giving you a straight answer.

The audit takes a few weeks and costs $2,500.

If you want to keep working together after that, we offer monthly advisory retainers at $1,500/month. Most clients use that time to work through implementation, troubleshoot what's not working, and figure out what to tackle next.

### Who we are

We're three former Microsoft veterans. Between us, we spent 25 years building and shipping technology at scale. For the last eight years we've been running a boutique IT consulting firm serving the biotech industry. We know what it looks like to actually deploy technology inside a real business, not just advise on it from the outside.

Barnabas Coaching is what we're doing because we think small businesses are getting bad AI advice and we can do better.

We work with businesses across Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, and the broader Puget Sound area. In-person when it makes sense, remote when it doesn't.

### Who this is for

Small businesses with 5 to 200 employees. Owner-operated or close to it. You know your business. You don't need us to explain what AI is. You need someone to look at your actual situation and tell you where it fits and where it doesn't.

If you're hoping AI will replace your entire staff or cut your costs in half overnight, we're probably not the right fit. If you want a realistic picture of what's possible and a clear starting point, we are.

---

### What the audit looks at

We go through your workflows, your tools, and your team's day-to-day. We're looking for three things:

1. Where are people doing repetitive work that software can handle?
2. Where are decisions being made slowly because information is scattered?
3. Where has AI already shown up in your industry, and is it working?

You get a written report with specific recommendations, ranked by effort and impact. Not a slide deck full of buzzwords. An actual plan.
