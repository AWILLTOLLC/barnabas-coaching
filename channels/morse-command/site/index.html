// The Science

The Koch Method, Gamified

Developed by German psychologist Ludwig Koch, this method is the gold standard for learning Morse Code — and the one recommended most by experienced amateur radio operators. We built an entire game around it.

Instead of slowing the code down (which teaches you wrong), Koch starts at full speed with just two characters. Master those, add one more. Your brain builds the right neural pathways from the start — the same way veteran CW operators learned.

01

Full Speed From Day One

Characters are always sent at real speed. No crutches, no bad habits to unlearn later.

02

Progressive Character Introduction

Start with two characters. Each zone adds more as you prove mastery through gameplay.

03

Instant Recognition, Not Counting

You learn to hear characters as sounds, not dots and dashes. The way real operators do it.

04

Adaptive Reinforcement

The AI targets your weak spots. Characters you miss appear more until they lock in permanently.
