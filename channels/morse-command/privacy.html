Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

Overview

Short version: Morse Command does not collect, store, or share your personal information. The iOS app collects no data whatsoever. The website collects only standard server logs.

This Privacy Policy describes how AWILLTO ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Morse Command iOS application and the morsecommand.com website.

We believe in privacy by default. We've built our product with that principle in mind — not as an afterthought.

The iOS App

The Morse Command iOS application collects no personal data of any kind.

  • No account creation or sign-in required
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising SDKs or third-party trackers
  • No crash reporting services that transmit data externally
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location
  • No data is transmitted from your device to our servers

All game data (progress, settings, scores) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

This Website

The morsecommand.com website is a static informational page. When you visit it, our web server automatically records standard server logs, which may include:

  • Your IP address
  • The date and time of your request
  • The page or resource you requested
  • Your browser type and operating system
  • The referring page (if you clicked a link to reach us)

These logs are collected automatically by our server (Caddy) for operational purposes only — to monitor uptime, diagnose technical issues, and protect against abuse. We do not use them to identify individuals or track behavior across sessions.

Server logs are retained only as long as operationally necessary and are not shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google's servers may log your IP address per their standard practices. You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

The iOS app uses no third-party services or SDKs that collect data.

Cookies & Tracking

We do not use cookies, web beacons, pixels, or any other tracking technologies on this website. No analytics services (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) are installed. No advertising networks have access to your visit.

Children's Privacy

Morse Command is suitable for all ages, including children. Because we collect no personal data, there is no special risk to children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete. If you have questions about your privacy, you're welcome to contact us — but there is genuinely no data on file about you.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. We will not retroactively change practices in ways that reduce your privacy without prominent notice.
