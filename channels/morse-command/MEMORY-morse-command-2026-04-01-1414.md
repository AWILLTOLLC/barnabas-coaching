# Memory: Reddit Due Diligence — 2026-04-01

## Incident
Aaron flagged that /r/preppers prohibits commercial posts. Dash had recommended it as a posting target without checking subreddit rules first. This was a failure of due diligence that could have gotten Aaron banned from his community.

## Standing Rule
Before recommending ANY subreddit as a channel:
1. Read the subreddit rules explicitly (especially Rule 1 or "self-promotion" rules)
2. Check karma/account age thresholds
3. Check whether educational/organic framing is genuinely permitted or just a loophole that gets called out
4. Only recommend subs where organic participation is the real angle — not promotion dressed up as content

## High-risk categories (commercial posts usually banned):
- /r/preppers — confirmed NO commercial posts
- Most hobby/enthusiast subs — assume no commercial unless verified

## Context
The prepper/ham community is skeptical of marketing. Spam flags cause lasting reputational damage. Treat Reddit as high-trust, low-tolerance. When in doubt: don't recommend it.
