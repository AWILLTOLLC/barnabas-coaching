Morse Command just launched on the App Store. It's an asteroid shooter built around the Koch method: enemy ships transmit in Morse, you decode and shoot. Same character order and accuracy thresholds your members already train with.

Aaron built it while going through Koch training himself. He wanted something that felt less like homework.
