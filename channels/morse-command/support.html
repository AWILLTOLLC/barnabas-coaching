Frequently Asked Questions

The Koch Method is a scientifically proven approach to learning Morse code, developed by German psychologist Ludwig Koch. Instead of starting with slow code and speeding up (which builds bad habits), it starts at full speed with just two characters. Once you can copy those at 90% accuracy, a new character is added. Your brain learns to hear Morse as sounds rather than counting dots and dashes — the way real operators do it.

Start at Zone 1, Level 1 — that's exactly what it's designed for. The game introduces characters gradually using the Koch Method. You'll start with just two characters and add more as you prove mastery. No prior Morse code experience needed. Don't worry about memorizing dots and dashes — just listen and respond.

Little Kid Mode is a play mode where every input registers as correct. Kids get the full visual and audio experience — lights, explosions, the works — without any frustration from wrong answers. It's designed for parents and grandparents to share the game with young kids who aren't ready for the real challenge yet. Great for building early familiarity with Morse sounds.

Power-ups unlock through combo streaks during gameplay. Hit an 8x combo for Slow Field (slows all enemies), 12x for EMP Blast (stuns all enemies), 15x for Repair Kit (restores base health), and 23x for the Nuke (clears the entire screen). They're designed as rewards for sustained accuracy under pressure — not a crutch for struggling through levels.
