### Post 3 — The "What I Actually Do" Post
*Tone: Concrete. Useful. Shows the work.*

---

When I do an AI audit for a small business, here's what a typical day looks like:

Morning: I sit down with the owner for 90 minutes. Not a survey — a real conversation about what the business does, what's breaking, and what they've already tried.

Then I follow someone around. A sales rep, an ops manager, a front desk person. I watch them work. I'm not asking what their process is — I'm watching what it actually is. Those are often different things.

What I'm looking for:
- Tasks that are repetitive and rule-based (automation candidates)
- Information that has to move between systems manually (integration problems)
- Decisions that follow a pattern but still require a human (augmentation opportunities)
- Places where the data exists but nobody's using it

By afternoon, I have a map. A week later, the owner has a written report with prioritized recommendations, specific tools worth evaluating, and an honest list of what I'm *not* recommending — and why.

That last section is usually what they remember.

$2,500. 7–10 business days. Starts with a free discovery call at barnabas.coach
