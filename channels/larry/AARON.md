Connection is produced, not assumed. The belief underneath: that showing up as-is, without output or effort, may not be enough to hold someone's interest.

The filter is real — most people don't meet the bar — but the bar may also be set high enough to protect against the risk of being seen and not wanted.

*   **What is missing:** Peer-level male friendships with people who are on their own growth trajectory. A values-aligned community (church or equivalent). People to admire and be sharpened by.
