# DECISIONS.md - Strategic Memory

_Log meaningful decisions here: what was decided, what was considered, why, and what was traded off. Prevents re-litigating the same choices._

---

## Format

```
### [YYYY-MM-DD] Decision title
**Decision:** What was decided
**Alternatives considered:** What else was on the table
**Why this path:** The reasoning
**Trade-offs accepted:** What we gave up
```

---

## Log

### [2026-02-21] House sale strategy: clean sale vs. Airbnb
**Decision:** Proceed with clean sale after repairs. No Airbnb.
**Alternatives considered:** Short-term Airbnb rental (3–12 months) leveraging two-unit layout (main floor + MIU suite) and hot tub.
**Why this path:** $8k/month carry cost, new listing penalty (no reviews = low occupancy), furnishing costs ~$10-20k, and Aaron doesn't want a fourth job. Timeline is 3–5 months to sale, not long enough to recoup Airbnb setup costs. Capital and energy better deployed elsewhere.
**Trade-offs accepted:** Leaving potential Airbnb revenue on the table. Accepted because the lifestyle cost (remote management of two units during a move) outweighed the financial upside on a short timeline.
