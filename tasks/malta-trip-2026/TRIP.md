## Rome — 3 Nights (Oct 3–5)

### Hotel: Donna Camilla Savelli ⭐
- 17th-century Baroque convent converted to 4-star hotel
- Rooftop terrace with Rome views, garden breakfast, vaulted ceilings
- Neighborhood: Trastevere (heart of it)
- Cost: ~€220–320/night
- Book direct: vretreats.com/donna-camilla-savelli
- **Request:** garden-facing room
- Condé Nast Traveler approved

### Airport → Hotel
- **Recommended:** Licensed white taxi from FCO arrivals — fixed flat rate €50 to Trastevere, no negotiating. Ignore anyone inside the terminal offering rides.
- **Alternative:** FL1 regional train FCO → Trastevere station (~€9pp, 45min, then 10-15min walk)

### Itinerary

**Oct 3 — Arrive & decompress**
- Land ~afternoon, taxi to Donna Camilla Savelli
- Easy wander through Trastevere cobblestone streets
- Aperitivo at a piazza bar (Piazza di Santa Maria in Trastevere is steps away)
- Pasta dinner — avoid any restaurant with a tourist photo menu outside
- Early to bed (jet lag)

**Oct 4 — Ancient Rome**
- Morning: Colosseum + Roman Forum + Palatine Hill (book timed entry online in advance — sells out)
- Afternoon: Walk along the Tiber, gelato, explore
- Evening: Trastevere neighborhood — comes alive after 8pm, best bars and restaurants

**Oct 5 — Vatican & Centro Storico**
- Early morning: Vatican Museums + Sistine Chapel (book timed entry online — essential)
- St. Peter's Basilica (free, no ticket needed but can be slow)
- Walk to Piazza Navona → Pantheon → Campo de' Fiori
- Final Italian dinner
- Early night — morning flight to Malta

### Practical Rome Notes
- **Language:** Don't worry. Trastevere is expat/tourist-friendly. English everywhere.
- **Key Italian:** Grazie (thank you), Il conto (the check), Un tavolo per due (table for two)
- **Tipping:** Not expected like in US. Round up or leave €1–2 is fine.
- **Cash:** Carry some euros. Many small spots prefer cash.
- **Pre-book:** Colosseum and Vatican both require advance tickets. Do this once flights are booked.

### Accommodation
- Already booked ✅

### Pre-Festival Exploration (Oct 6–8)
- **Valletta** — Malta's capital, UNESCO World Heritage, stunning Baroque architecture, St. John's Co-Cathedral (Caravaggio's finest work inside)
- **Mdina** — "The Silent City," ancient walled hilltop town. Go at dusk. Surreal.
- **Blue Lagoon (Comino Island)** — One of the most beautiful swimming spots in the Mediterranean. Go early/weekday to beat crowds. Ferry from Cirkewwa (~€15pp roundtrip).

### Post-Festival Exploration (Oct 12–15)
- **Gozo Island** — Ferry from Ċirkewwa. More relaxed, rural, beautiful. Azure Window area, Xlendi Bay.
- **The Three Cities** (Vittoriosa, Senglea, Cospicua) — Medieval Knights Hospitaller fortifications, stunning harbor views, fewer tourists than Valletta.
- **Marsaxlokk** — Fishing village, colorful luzzu boats, great fresh seafood on Sunday market days.
- **Temple of Hagar Qim** — 5,000-year-old megalithic temples, older than Stonehenge and the Pyramids.

### Malta Practical Notes
- **Language:** English is an official language. No issues anywhere.
- **Currency:** Euro
- **Getting around:** Buses are cheap but slow. Taxis and Bolt (Uber equivalent) are easy.
- **Weather in October:** ~25°C / 77°F, sunny, warm sea. Perfect.
- **Driving:** Left side of the road (British influence). Rental car useful for post-festival island exploration.
