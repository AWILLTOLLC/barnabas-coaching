## 🟡 DO IN APRIL–JUNE 2026

### Tickets & Advance Bookings
- [ ] Book **Colosseum + Roman Forum + Palatine Hill** timed entry — coopculture.it. Goes on sale ~3 months in advance. Book for morning of Oct 4. Sells out fast.
- [ ] Book **Vatican Museums + Sistine Chapel** timed entry — vatican.va. Book for early morning Oct 5 (8am slot if available). These sell out weeks in advance.

### Financial Prep
- [ ] Notify your bank and credit cards of travel dates: Italy (Oct 2–6) and Malta (Oct 6–16).
- [ ] Check your cards for **foreign transaction fees** — if your card charges 3%, get a no-fee travel card (Chase Sapphire, Capital One Venture, or similar). Apply now if needed.
- [ ] Consider getting a **Charles Schwab debit card** — refunds all international ATM fees. Great for cash in Europe.

### ETIAS (EU Travel Authorization)
- [ ] Register for **ETIAS** for both Aaron and Lily — travel-europe.europa.eu/etias_en. ~€7 each, valid 3 years, required for US citizens entering Schengen. Takes 10 minutes, usually approved instantly but can take up to 30 days — don't do this last minute.

## 🔵 DO 2–4 WEEKS BEFORE (September 2026)

### Pack & Prepare
- [ ] **Packing check for climate:** Rome Oct 3–6 (~72°F, light layers for evening). Malta Oct 6–16 (~77°F, warm, Mediterranean). Festival nights can be cooler — bring one light jacket. Pack light — you're gone 13 days, not 30.
- [ ] **Festival outfit planning** — Malta venues are outdoor/open-air. Comfortable shoes for dancing are non-negotiable. Layers for late nights.
- [ ] **Bring Glimmer Cards** to Anjunadeep — pack a stack. Give them out together at the festival.
- [ ] **Pack a small day bag** for Rome sightseeing — pickpockets exist, keep valuables in front pockets or a crossbody bag.
- [ ] **Earplugs** — for the festival (protect your hearing, still hear the music perfectly).
- [ ] **Buy euros** — Get €300–400 cash each before leaving. Your bank will have better rates than the airport. Small bills preferred.

### Health
- [ ] **Jet lag strategy** — Start shifting sleep 2–3 days before departure. Night flight to Rome: stay awake on the plane, sleep 6–7 hours, arrive afternoon, push through to 10pm local time. Light exposure in the morning. This makes Day 1 functional.
- [ ] **Travel meds kit** — Ibuprofen/Tylenol, Pepto, anti-diarrheal, blister bandages, any prescription meds with enough supply + a few extra days.
- [ ] **Travel size toiletries** — TSA compliant if carry-on only, or just check a bag.

### Documents
- [ ] **Make digital copies of everything** — passports, ETIAS confirmations, flight confirmations, hotel confirmations. Save to Google Drive or email to yourself. Screenshot everything.
- [ ] **Print physical backup copies** — one set each. Keep separately from originals.
