## Notes for RFQ Outreach

**What to include in your RFQ email:**
- Product name: 1" Scotch-Eye Auger Bit
- Material: High carbon steel (1075 or equivalent)
- Finish: Black oxide
- Geometry: Helical flute, lead screw tip
- Handle: Welded scotch-eye socket (forged ring eye for wooden handle)
- Quantity: 1,000 units (with potential for repeat orders)
- Budget target: $8–15/unit FOB
- Request: Technical feasibility + sample quote

**Key manufacturing capabilities needed:**
1. Steel forging or machining (helical flute)
2. Threading/forming for lead screw tip
3. Welding or forging the scotch-eye socket
4. Black oxide surface treatment

**India note:** Jalandhar is the hand tool capital of India. Companies there routinely do custom OEM for global buyers. Minimum orders are often negotiable at 500–1,000 pcs.

**Pakistan note:** Wazirabad and Sialkot regions have deep forging heritage. Fasnna Group is the most professionally organized Pakistan manufacturer found.

**Ukraine note:** Given ongoing war, factor shipping/lead time risk. Quality of Ukrainian forged tools (STRYI, BeaverCraft, Sharky) is genuinely excellent.

**Taiwan note:** Best for precision, consistency, established export processes. Pricing likely toward upper end ($12–18/unit) but quality highest.

**USA note:** Warwood and Baucor both do custom. Expect $25–50+/unit but worth getting quotes for comparison and as domestic fallback.
