Most owners already know they need to be doing something with AI. The hard part is figuring out

 One-Time Engagement

## AI Audit
 $2,500 one-time

You've heard a lot about what AI can do. But what can it actually do for your business,
 in your specific workflows, with your team and budget? That's the question the AI Audit answers.

Aaron maps your existing operations, identifies where AI can create
 real returns, and delivers a written report you can act on immediately, or hand to your team.
 For Seattle-area clients, the intake and workflow mapping can be done on-site.

The report isn't a generic AI overview. It's specific to your business: the tools, the vendors,
 the workflows, and the sequence that makes sense for where you are right now.

 [Get Started →](/contact)

### What's included

- ✓ Intake interview: A structured 90-minute conversation about your business, your team, your current tools, and your biggest operational pain points.
- ✓ Workflow mapping: Aaron documents your core processes to identify where time is being lost and where AI can compress or eliminate manual work.
- ✓ Written assessment report: A clear, jargon-free document covering findings, opportunities, and risks. Written for an owner, not a CTO.
- ✓ Prioritized recommendations: Not a list of every possible AI application. A ranked action plan based on effort, impact, and your actual priorities.
- ✓ Tool & vendor guidance: Specific recommendations on what to use, with context on tradeoffs, costs, and implementation complexity.
- ✓ Debrief call: A final 60-minute call to walk through the report, answer questions, and discuss next steps.

- → Owner-operated businesses with 5–100 employees
- → You know AI is important but not sure where to start
- → You want an objective view, with no vendor pitch attached
- → You've tried a few tools but haven't seen traction
- → You want a concrete plan you can actually execute

 Ongoing Support

## Common questions

### Do I need to start with the Audit before the Retainer?
 Not required, but often recommended. The Audit gives both of us a clear picture of your business before we dive into ongoing strategy work. If you've already done this work internally or with another advisor, we can start straight with the Retainer.

### What industries do you work with?
 Aaron has the deepest experience in biotech, professional services, and technology-adjacent businesses. That said, AI strategy translates across industries. The workflow assessment approach works whether you run a law firm, a construction company, or a healthcare practice.

### Do you do implementation work, or just strategy?
 Primarily strategy: helping you make the right decisions, then execute them with your team or with vendors Aaron can recommend. For specific implementation needs, he can point you to the right resources.

### How quickly can we get started?
 Discovery calls are typically available within a week. The AI Audit begins after a brief intake and scoping process. Coaching Retainers start on the first of the following month.
