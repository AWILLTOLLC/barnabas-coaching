## Content Format Notes

**Aaron's post structure (based on voice profile):**
1. Open with the specific claim or problem — no warm-up
2. 2-3 paragraphs of real mechanics (numbers, named tools, named patterns)
3. The thing most consultants won't say — this is the differentiator
4. CTA: soft ("if you want to think this through, my calendar is open") not hard

**What to avoid:**
- Generic AI hype ("AI is transforming everything")
- Lists of 10 tools with no opinion on any of them
- Anything that sounds like a vendor brochure

**Internal linking map:**
- Every post → links to "5 AI Mistakes" (already live)
- Every Tier 1 post → links to discovery call booking
- Tier 2 posts → link to relevant Tier 1 posts
