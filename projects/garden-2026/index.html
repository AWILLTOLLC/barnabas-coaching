🛒 Recommended Starts to Buy

🗓 Buy Schedule Summary:   April → chives, lemon thyme (Urban Earth or Sky)  |  Early May → Abundant Greens tomato sale — go early, best varieties sell out fast  |  May 15+ → transplant everything warm-season outdoors
★ Top Priority

🍅 Tomato (1–2 plants)

Best for Seattle (early, under 70 days):
· Stupice — Czech heirloom, 55–65 days, sweet, very reliable in PNW cool summers. Most commonly available.
· Siletz — bred for the PNW, 52 days, sets fruit even in cool/cloudy weather. Easy to find.
· Sungold (cherry) — 57 days, incredibly sweet orange cherry. Widely available and incredibly productive.
· Black Krim — 69 days, rich/smoky flavor, available at Abundant Greens.

Avoid anything 80+ days — not enough summer heat in Seattle.

📍 Abundant Greens, early May · $4.50/plant
★ Top Priority

🌿 Basil

Best varieties:
· Genovese — the classic, widely available everywhere, big flavorful leaves.
· Italian Large Leaf — same family, easy to find at any nursery.

Plant near tomatoes (companion). Hard to grow from seed without steady heat. Cheap start, big payoff.

📍 Urban Earth (Fremont) or Sky Nursery · mid-May
◆ High Value

🫑 Shishito Pepper

Variety: Just labeled "Shishito" — it's a single variety, you won't see sub-varieties at most nurseries.

Easy, prolific, delicious blistered in a pan with flaky salt. One plant produces all season long in Seattle.

📍 Abundant Greens or Urban Earth · mid-May · $4.50
◆ High Value

🌱 Lemon Thyme

Look for: Thymus citriodorus — sold simply as "Lemon Thyme" at most herb sections.

Perennial — plant once, harvest forever. Hardy through Seattle winters. Small footprint, huge culinary payoff.

📍 Urban Earth Nursery, Fremont · April
◆ High Value

🌾 Chives

Common variety: Standard chives (Allium schoenoprasum) — available at every nursery in spring.

Perennial, zero maintenance, comes back every year. Use constantly. Gets pretty purple flowers too.

📍 Urban Earth Nursery or Sky · April
· Optional

🌸 Nasturtiums

Look for: Tropaeolum majus — "Jewel Mix" or "Alaska" (variegated leaf) are common and attractive.

Edible flowers + leaves, attract pollinators for your cucumber, and they're beautiful. Can direct sow from seed or buy a start.

📍 Any nursery · direct sow or start · April
