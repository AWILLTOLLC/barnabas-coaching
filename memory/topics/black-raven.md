# Black Raven Company

Black Raven Co. is Aaron's auger manufacturing business, producing high-quality 1" Scotch-Eye auger bits for bushcrafters and green woodworkers. The company has sold over 5,000 augers to customers in 30+ countries and offers a lifetime warranty on all products. The augers feature high carbon steel construction with a welded forged ring eye for wooden handles, targeting the $8–15/unit FOB price point.
