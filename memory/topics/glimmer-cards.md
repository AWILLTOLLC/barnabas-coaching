# Glimmer Cards

Glimmer Cards is Aaron's festival card business, producing small G-rated compliment cards designed for raves and EDM festivals. The Core Pack contains 10 unique cards priced at $9.99 + shipping. The concept centers on "glimmers" — small moments of unexpected joy and connection that make people feel safe, seen, and warmly human. Cards are paper-based, recyclable, and designed to be intentionally given to specific people at shows.
