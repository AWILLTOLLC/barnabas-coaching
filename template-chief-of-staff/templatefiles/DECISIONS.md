# DECISIONS.md — Decision Log

Purpose: record meaningful decisions with reasoning so they never get re-litigated. Append one entry per decision. Newest at the bottom.

---

## Format (EXAMPLE — do not keep this entry)

```
### [YYYY-MM-DD] Short title
**Decision:** What was decided, in one line.
**Alternatives considered:** The main options on the table.
**Trade-offs accepted:** What we gave up and why it was worth it.
**Context:** Anything a future session needs to understand why.
```
